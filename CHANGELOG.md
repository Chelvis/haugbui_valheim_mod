# Changelog

## 0.0.12

A tomb checkbox, off by default, keeps that Haugbui's area loaded while he can be out and you are away, so the
same Haugbui keeps working. Rest, a revive wait, and daytime without the Altar do not keep it loaded. Closing the
game still pauses the world. The mark beside the checkbox warns that this uses more CPU.

Reset on a station panel zeros the supplied and produced counts and runs that same finite amount again. The tomb
panel has Find Haugbui, and the tomb hover shows Shift+F for the same look and the 5 second silhouette. The key
does not run while that panel is open.

## 0.0.11

The bag fills to 80%, then feeds machines from what it already carries and stores the rest. Every 3 real minutes, and
first after it leaves the tomb, emptying the bag is the next task. Damage with no creature behind it, such as eitr, a
stake wall or fire, does not scare it or cancel the trip. Shift+C and Shift+V copy and paste a chest filter or a
delivery request, from the editor and from the chest hover. Offence and defence are 0.5, 0.8, 1.1, 1.4, 1.7, 2.0.
