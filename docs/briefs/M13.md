## 3. Product rules

### HB-T-118 — material piles (HB-176, confirmed 2026-11-02)

Tomb toggle, default **off**. A pile is a player-built piece whose recipe is one stack of one transportable
material (wood, stone, marble, metal and the like). Furniture, chests and other buildings are not piles. A pile in
a ward the owner cannot use is left alone.

While the toggle is on, he dismantles a pile only to feed a delivery or a production job. The refund is received
before the piece is removed. What does not fit the carry limit becomes a ground drop and is collected in parts.
Per tomb, either piles come before chests, or chests come first and a pile is used only when the chests are short.
Carried items stay first. Ground order does not change.

### HB-T-119 — keep lit (HB-177, confirmed 2026-11-04)

On a `Fireplace` the player can refill (`m_canRefill`, and not `m_infiniteFuel`), Shift+E only toggles keep-lit on
that piece. Every such fire starts **off**. The hover shows
`[Shift+E] keep lit (off)` / `[Shift+E] manter aceso (desligado)` until he turns it on, then the on line.

While on, refill when the shown fuel is **1 or 0**, up to full, with that fire's own fuel item, through the game's
own add-fuel call. Remove the unit from the source only after the fire's fuel rises. Sources follow HB-113.
A fire already above 1 is left alone. Smelters, kilns, ovens and the shield generator are not this task.
Wards and territories apply.

### HB-T-122 — Frigid kiln amount (HB-181, confirmed 2026-11-09)

The panel currently forces Infinite because the kiln has no ore queue. Kelvin wants Finite too: produce the requested number, then stop, so the kiln does not empty the base of its fuel. Same finite target as the charcoal kiln (products of this job). The hot tub and the shield generator stay infinite-only.
